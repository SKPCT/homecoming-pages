Privacy Policy

Your adventure stays with you. Here is what Homecoming stores on your device, what may sync through your own iCloud account, and what happens when you choose to purchase.

Who is responsible

Homecoming is published under the SkyPact organization. The legal entity responsible for the processing described here is SkyPact LLC, registered in Florida, United States, at 5401 S. Kirkman Rd., Suite 310, Orlando, FL 32819, United States. Privacy requests: admin@skypactgroup.com. General support: admin@skypactgroup.com.

This policy covers the Homecoming iOS app, including its free Sea, Road and Space journeys and the optional Battle Royal Desert and Jungle expansion. It also covers messages you send directly to SkyPact about the app and the policy and support pages. Apple operates its own services under its own privacy notices.

What stays on your device

Homecoming does not collect player data from the app for SkyPact or third-party partners. It does not include third-party SDKs or analytics software. It does not require a game account and has no player chat, public leaderboard or developer-operated gameplay server. We do not upload your saved levels, scores, diamonds or settings to a SkyPact server.

The app keeps progress, best scores, preferences, flags for the launch story, free tutorial and Battle Royal story already seen, a history of Battle Royal milestone offers already shown, and a recovery copy in its local app storage so that you can continue playing. Device backups may include those files according to your Apple settings. The optional iCloud save contains the same game records, including preferences; ordinary progress merges keep each device’s preferences. Reset progress clears the free and paid campaign records and their recovery copy, while keeping settings on the device where you reset, purchase rights and the saved milestone-offer history so the same offer is not repeated. When iCloud is on for Homecoming, a reset also replaces older progress on other devices when the reset save arrives and they return to a safe menu. Deleting the app removes its local app data but does not by itself remove an iCloud save; offloading an app or retaining a device backup can also keep data. Manage device backups separately in Apple settings.

The launch story, free tutorial and Battle Royal story have separate seen flags on each device. Those flag values may be included in the private iCloud save, but receiving another device’s save never replaces this device’s three flags. Journey and chapter introduction history and the free-campaign celebration record can still sync.

When iCloud is enabled for Homecoming, progress may also be stored in your own iCloud account. SkyPact cannot access that private save. To clear it, use Reset progress in Homecoming Settings. Once the reset has synced, the iCloud save holds no missions, scores, diamonds, introduction history or celebration record. It keeps only your game preferences, the history of Battle Royal milestone offers already shown, and technical details of the reset itself: a counter, a time and the save format version. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Homecoming in your device’s Apple settings.

Purchase rights are verified on your device using Apple’s StoreKit service. When certain purchase or save errors occur, the app writes a short technical entry to the local device log: the step that failed, a numeric code and, for save errors, an error category. These entries contain no account information. The app does not upload this log or other gameplay diagnostics to SkyPact. Optional support messages and website visits are separate activities described below.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles payment, tax and App Store account information. The app receives StoreKit purchase and entitlement information, such as the product, transaction status and whether access has been revoked, to unlock or restore Battle Royal. It verifies that information on the device. SkyPact does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password through the app.

Apple may provide SkyPact with sales, financial, crash or App Store reports under Apple’s platform settings and agreements. These are separate from the app’s local and private iCloud progress saves. Please do not send passwords, full payment details or identity documents when requesting support.

Age range and younger players

Homecoming is intended for players aged 13 and older, with no upper age limit. The App Store age rating describes game content and may differ by region and operating-system version. It does not replace local privacy rules or parental permission. We do not ask for a birth date, identity document or photograph in the app.

Some laws require apps to check a player’s age category. When your device’s software supports that check, Apple tells the game whether such a law applies to your account. If one does, Homecoming asks Apple for your age category, such as under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or 18 and over. Apple then shares an age range, how that age was declared or confirmed, and whether certain parental controls apply. It does not share your birth date. The game uses this information on your device while it is open and does not save it or send it to SkyPact.

Being 13 or older does not remove a requirement for parental permission where local law requires it for consent-based processing.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to SkyPact, contact admin@skypactgroup.com so we can investigate and remove information where appropriate. We do not use a purchase, agreement to these terms or an age rating as parental consent to collect a child’s personal information.

Support information and permissions

If you contact SkyPact, we receive your address, message and any information you choose to attach. We use it to answer you, diagnose the reported issue and handle privacy or purchase questions. Send only what is needed; screenshots can contain personal information.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photo-library, precise location or notification permissions. It uses your own iCloud account for optional progress sync and has no game account, social sign-in or SkyPact-operated cloud-save service. Opening a support link or a policy link takes you to a separate service, which may receive ordinary network and browser information under its own notice.

These policy and support pages contain no forms, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Opening a page still sends ordinary network information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to the website host to deliver and protect the service. The host may keep operational and security logs under its service settings. Contact admin@skypactgroup.com for information about the hosting provider and applicable retention.

Why information is used

For people in the EEA, UK and other places requiring a lawful basis: local game functions and purchase checks support providing the game you request; our legitimate interests support responding to support messages and preventing abuse; legal obligations support required accounting and privacy records and any age check the law requires. We assess interests against your rights and do not substitute legitimate interests for consent where consent is required.

Apple describes its own purposes and legal bases in its linked notice. The game does not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Recipients and international processing

SkyPact gives access to support information only to people and service providers who need it to handle the request or a legal obligation. Apple processes purchases and platform services. We may disclose information if lawfully required, to protect rights or investigate abuse, with appropriate safeguards; we do not publish private support messages.

Apple and any support provider may operate in countries other than your own. Where SkyPact arranges a restricted transfer, it will use a lawful transfer mechanism and required safeguards. The linked provider notices describe their transfer arrangements. Contact admin@skypactgroup.com to ask about applicable safeguards or obtain information about them.

How long information is kept

Local progress remains until reset, app-data deletion or replacement by later play or a merged save; device backups follow your Apple settings. An iCloud save can remain after the app is deleted. Use Reset progress before deleting the app if you want the iCloud save cleared. A reset replaces older campaign progress when its reset save reaches another device; it does not delete Apple’s purchase records.

SkyPact keeps ordinary support correspondence for up to 12 months after a request closes, then deletes or anonymizes it, unless a dispute, security investigation or law requires longer. Accounting and legal records are kept for the applicable mandatory period. Apple applies its own published retention rules; resetting game progress does not delete its records.
