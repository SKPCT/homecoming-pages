The free game and Battle Royal

Sea, Road and Space are all available from the start, with 20 free missions in each. Journeys can be played independently in any order. Within each journey, complete a level to unlock the next and replay completed levels at any time. Completion counts at either Standard or Relaxed pace. Existing completed levels and scores are kept; levels up to and including the first incomplete level are available, and any later completed level remains replayable.

Every finished level 20 follows its arrival with a 15-second homecoming cutscene for that journey, including replays. Sea celebrates at a seaside cottage, Road with fireworks, and Space on Earth. Skip appears after three seconds. Sea can suggest the Road map and Road can suggest the Space map when that next journey is not complete; Back to levels returns to the current map. The sixtieth free completion adds a five-second grand celebration, including when an older sparse save finishes on another mission. The grand celebration offers Back to journeys, plus Play Battle Royal for owners. Any Battle Royal invitation remains optional and appears after the cutscene.

Battle Royal is a separate optional expansion with 30 Desert and 30 Jungle missions and action combat. It can be purchased without finishing the free missions. Its chapters and levels progress through play. The first Battle Royal opening after verified ownership presents a skippable introduction to the Desert and Jungle adventure; it does not unlock additional levels. The Battle Royal story is remembered separately on each device; synced mission progress does not mark it as seen. Every Desert 30 victory continues from the finish star and arrival into a short desert ending: Enter the jungle opens the Jungle map, and Back to battles returns to Battle Royal. Every Jungle 30 victory continues into Curiosity’s final homecoming, with Back to battles returning to Battle Royal or Back to Homecoming returning to the adventure chooser. Skip appears after three seconds. Defeat or leaving a battle does not trigger an ending. Replaying an ending from Settings does not award a score or unlock a mission.

Scores, diamonds and completion marks are game records with no cash value. The game has no wagering, paid random loot boxes, tradable virtual currency or real-world prizes.

On a fresh installation, a skippable 42-second story introduces Curiosity’s adventures, followed by a skippable 34-second tutorial before the adventure chooser. Skip appears after two seconds in each. Finishing or skipping records that sequence as seen on this device. Receiving iCloud progress does not skip these introductions on a new device. If only the tutorial remains unseen on a later launch, Play free presents it before the free hub; otherwise Play free opens the hub directly. Watching or skipping these sequences does not unlock missions or purchases.

Saved progress and practical limits

Progress and settings are stored on the device with a local recovery copy. When iCloud is enabled for Homecoming, the app also stores a private save in your own iCloud account. SkyPact cannot access that save and does not operate a game-save server. Available progress from another device can be applied when Homecoming starts or while you are on the adventure chooser, a free journey selection or level map, or the Battle Royal hub or map. Incoming changes wait while a level, introduction, story, ending, Settings or policy page, or purchase operation is active. The game saves the merged result on this device before showing it. Apple controls delivery timing, so sync is not immediate or guaranteed. Compatible saves combine completed missions, diamonds, journey and chapter introduction records, milestone-offer history and the free-campaign celebration record, and keep the best score for each record. The launch story, tutorial and Battle Royal story flags stay local to each device. A newer Reset progress replaces older campaign progress when the reset save arrives and another device returns to a safe menu; that receiving device keeps its three local intro flags. Deleting or reinstalling the app does not guarantee recovery, and deleting local app data does not itself delete an iCloud save. Device backups are separate and follow your Apple settings.
