Support

Help Curiosity find the way home.

Get help

Contact admin@skypactgroup.com for gameplay, purchase-access or accessibility help. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or identity documents.

Unlock free journey levels

Sea, Road and Space are all available from the start and can be played in any order. Within each journey, complete the previous level to unlock the next, then use Next level after a win or return to the level map. A locked level shows a lock and “Complete the previous level”. Completed levels remain replayable.

Completion is shared between Standard and Relaxed pace, while best scores stay separate by pace. Switching pace does not relock a level. No purchase is required to unlock any free level.

When updating from an earlier version, your completed levels and scores are preserved. Within each journey, levels up to and including the first incomplete level are open, and any later level already marked complete remains replayable. Reset progress returns each journey to level 1 and does not remove Battle Royal purchase rights.

Every finished level 20 follows its arrival with a 15-second homecoming cutscene for that journey, including replays. Sea celebrates at a seaside cottage, Road with fireworks, and Space on Earth. Skip appears after three seconds. Sea can suggest the Road map and Road can suggest the Space map when that next journey is not complete; Back to levels returns to the current map. The sixtieth free completion adds a five-second grand celebration, including when an older sparse save finishes on another mission. The grand celebration offers Back to journeys, plus Play Battle Royal for owners. Any Battle Royal invitation remains optional and appears after the cutscene. Seeing or skipping the grand beat records the campaign-celebration flag; ordinary journey homecomings continue to replay. A Battle Royal invitation after free level 10 or 20 appears at most once per journey and level; Continue free journey preserves the chosen destination without requiring purchase.

Curiosity's stories

On a fresh installation, a skippable 42-second story introduces Curiosity’s adventures, followed by a skippable 34-second tutorial before the adventure chooser. Skip appears after two seconds in each. Finishing or skipping records that sequence as seen on this device. Receiving iCloud progress does not skip these introductions on a new device. If only the tutorial remains unseen on a later launch, Play free presents it before the free hub; otherwise Play free opens the hub directly.

Settings → Curiosity's stories offers Watch the story and Watch the tutorial, plus Watch the Battle Royal story for owners. Watch the sea homecoming, Watch the road homecoming and Watch the space homecoming each appear once that journey’s level 20 is complete. Homecoming replays include the grand celebration when all 60 free missions are complete. Watch the desert ending appears after Desert 30 is complete, and Watch the jungle finale appears after Jungle 30 is complete. Their availability follows saved Battle Royal progress; no separate ending-seen flag is stored. Every replay returns to Settings and changes no scores, progress or first-time flags. Reset progress clears the launch-story, tutorial and Battle Royal story flags on the device where you reset, along with campaign progress; it keeps settings and purchase ownership. A reset received from iCloud does not replace these three local intro flags.

Restore Battle Royal

Use the Apple Account that purchased Battle Royal for Media & Purchases on your device, then open Homecoming Settings → Restore purchase. The game may recognize ownership automatically. A completed restore does not charge you again.

An unlocked purchase gives access to the Battle Royal campaign. Desert missions progress through play; Jungle unlocks after Desert is completed. Restore purchase restores access through the Apple Account used for Media & Purchases; it does not transfer mission progress or scores. Progress may sync separately through the device’s iCloud account when enabled for Homecoming. The first Battle Royal opening with verified ownership and an unseen Battle Royal story flag presents a 36-second story; Skip is available after two seconds, and Begin or Skip continues to the Battle Royal hub. The Battle Royal story’s seen flag stays local to this device, even when mission progress arrives from iCloud. Chapter introduction history can still sync. Chapter introductions still appear when first entering each chapter.

If access still appears locked, check your connection, confirm the purchasing Apple Account, close and reopen the app, then try Restore purchase again. Contact admin@skypactgroup.com if the problem continues. Share the error text, app version and device model, but not your password or payment information.

After a Desert 30 victory, Enter the jungle opens its newly unlocked map. After a Jungle 30 victory, the finale offers Back to battles and Back to Homecoming. These endings also play on later victories of those missions; Skip is available after three seconds.

Apple purchase help

Progress on another device

Use the same Apple Account for iCloud on both devices and turn on iCloud for Homecoming. After playing on one device, leave the other on the adventure chooser or a journey or Battle Royal map and allow time for Apple to deliver the save. Incoming progress waits until you leave gameplay, introductions, stories or endings and close any open Settings, policy page or purchase sheet. The game saves the merged result locally before showing it. This is separate from the Apple Account used for Media & Purchases and Restore purchase. If progress has not appeared, check iCloud and the connection; fully closing and reopening Homecoming can retry loading the available save. Local play remains available when iCloud is unavailable. Sync is not instant or guaranteed, and SkyPact cannot inspect or recover your private save. The launch story, tutorial and Battle Royal story are remembered per device even when mission progress arrives from iCloud. Reset progress can replace older campaign progress on your other devices, while preserving their three local intro flags; deleting the app alone does not delete its iCloud data.

Apple billing and refunds

Apple handles the payment. To request a refund for an eligible purchase, use Apple’s refund process below. Homecoming version 1 does not include a separate in-app refund-request button. Refund eligibility is governed by applicable law and Apple’s process.

A refund or revoked purchase can remove Battle Royal access. It does not turn a local progress reset into a refund. Contact admin@skypactgroup.com if you need help understanding a purchase-access problem.
